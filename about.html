About the project

HUWiki — an idea that grew

HUWiki is developed by Måns Mårtensson, a teacher at Himmerlands Efterskole in Denmark.

After many years of using TiddlyWiki, the idea was to make it easier to collaborate on wikis, share content, and discover new ways of learning and creating.

The original HUWiki edition was developed for the school's Google Workspace environment. HUWiki Personal is an experiment in making similar capabilities available to ordinary Google accounts.

The name HUWiki comes from its beginnings as a school-based wiki project in Denmark. HUWiki is independent and is not affiliated with Wikipedia, Wikimedia, or the Hungarian-language Wikipedia.

A personal, non-commercial project

The project is developed as a labour of love, with the help of language models for tasks including programming and testing. It is still under development and free during the current preview phase.

Danish first — more languages later

HUWiki Personal currently has a Danish interface. English and possibly other languages are planned.
