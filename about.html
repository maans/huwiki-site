About the project

HUWiki — an idea that grew

HUWiki is developed by Måns Mårtensson, a teacher at Himmerlands Efterskole in Denmark.

After many years of using TiddlyWiki, the idea was to make it easier to collaborate on wikis, share content, and discover new ways of learning and creating.

The original HUWiki edition was developed for the school's Google Workspace environment. HUWiki is an experiment in making similar capabilities available to ordinary Google accounts.

The name HUWiki comes from its beginnings as a school-based wiki project in Denmark. HUWiki is independent and is not affiliated with Wikipedia, Wikimedia, or the Hungarian-language Wikipedia.

A personal, non-commercial project

The project is developed as a labour of love, with help from language models for tasks including programming and testing. HUWiki is free to use and will always remain free. There will be no paid editions, subscriptions or commercial versions. Development is ongoing, but the commitment to free access is permanent.

Languages

HUWiki includes Danish and English interfaces. Language coverage varies between editions and plugins; the application remains under active development.
