Privacy policy

Working draft · 19 October 2026

Important: This is a working draft, not the final reviewed privacy policy for HUWiki Personal. The current OAuth scopes, integrations, storage, deletion features, and any external services must be verified before this becomes the official policy or is submitted for Google's OAuth verification.

Who is responsible?

HUWiki Personal is a non-commercial development project by Måns Mårtensson. Contact: humamamm@gmail.com.

This public information website

This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Its own code contains no advertising, analytics, or data-collection forms. GitHub may process technical visitor data as part of hosting; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The HUWiki Personal application

HUWiki Personal runs separately through Google Apps Script and uses Google account authentication and Google Drive features. Wikis can be stored and shared within the user's Google environment. The exact Google permissions requested by the current version are still being reviewed.

Integrations and sharing

HUWiki is being developed with optional extensions and integrations. Any use of external services, including AI providers, will be documented after the behaviour of the current release is verified. Users should avoid putting sensitive information into preview wikis until those details have been documented.

Access, retention and deletion

The reviewed version of this policy will describe the exact storage locations, sharing controls, logging, retention periods, and deletion capabilities. Please contact the project if you have questions.

Google data

Google accounts and Google Drive are also subject to Google's own terms and privacy policy. See Google's privacy policy.
