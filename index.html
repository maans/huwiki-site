TiddlyWiki · Google Apps Script · Collaboration

Your wiki. Your ideas. More possibilities.

HUWiki helps people create, organise, and share TiddlyWikis in an environment built with Google Apps Script and Google Drive.

Built on TiddlyWiki, created by Jeremy Ruston and a worldwide community.

Open HUWiki ↗About the project
Preview release. HUWiki includes Danish and English interfaces, with language coverage varying between editions and plugins. A Google account is required. The OAuth testing release is limited to registered test users and has not yet been publicly verified by Google.

A wiki that grows with your ideas

HUWiki builds on TiddlyWiki's flexible model of small, interconnected pieces of information — adding ways to create, share, and extend wikis.

◈

Create and organise

Build wikis for learning, projects, documentation, knowledge, and creative work.

Share and collaborate

✳

Extend and publish

Explore specialised wiki editions, plugins, and ways to publish your content.

Made by a long-time TiddlyWiki enthusiast

HUWiki is a personal project by Måns Mårtensson, a teacher at Himmerlands Efterskole in Denmark. The aim is to make TiddlyWiki easier to explore, both individually and with others.

HUWiki is free to use and will always remain free. No paid editions, subscriptions, or commercial versions are planned or offered. HUWiki is still under active development.

Questions or feedback? Email humamamm@gmail.com
