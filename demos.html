See HUWiki in action

Short walkthroughs of real HUWiki projects: from notes to a plan, a puppet to a performance, and a catalog to an order.

Three projects to explore

AI Edition

From notes to a garden plan

Follow a community garden example from wiki notes to a conversation with a local Gemma model, then review the reply and turn it into a wiki page. The demo also shows how to check and use AI-assisted WikiText.

Animation Edition

A little world in motion

See how a puppet, two scenes and a change of light come together in a short theatre performance. The Cutout workshops use editable wiki content to build and publish the show.

Watch on YouTube ↗

Shop Edition

Clay & Coast: from catalog to order

Explore a fictional ceramics shop. Set up product variants, publish a catalog and use Google Forms to receive requests in the private order book. This example takes no payments.
